iCloud 同步

在“设置 → 数据与安全”中检查 iCloud 同步状态。关闭同步不会删除本机或 iCloud 中已有的记录；重新开启后，App 会继续使用你的 iCloud 私有数据库同步。

提醒与桌面小组件

提醒可在“设置 → 提醒”中管理；系统通知权限可前往 iOS 设置调整。桌面小组件的添加和显示方式说明位于“设置 → 偏好 → 桌面小组件”。

导入、导出与隐私

数据导入和导出位于“设置 → 数据与安全”。有关数据存储、同步和删除方式，请查看隐私政策。

iCloud 同步

提醒與桌面小工具

匯入、匯出與隱私

iCloud Sync

Check iCloud sync in Settings → Data & Security. Turning sync off does not delete records already stored on your device or in iCloud. When you turn it on again, the app resumes syncing through your private iCloud database.

Reminders and Home Screen Widgets

Manage reminders in Settings → Reminders. Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings. Instructions for adding and configuring widgets are in Settings → Preferences → Home Screen Widgets.

Import, Export, and Privacy

Import and export tools are in Settings → Data & Security. For details about storage, sync, and deletion, read the Privacy Policy.
